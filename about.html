About MDC

Who we are and what we stand for

Our Story

Meta Developer Community (MDC) at GITAM Visakhapatnam started with a simple idea — bring together students who love building things. What began as a small group of curious developers has grown into a thriving community focused on learning, collaboration, and shipping real projects.

Our Mission

We exist to make technology approachable for every student, no matter their starting point. Through workshops, hackathons, and peer mentorship, we help members go from writing their first line of code to building products that solve real problems.

Curiosity

We ask questions, explore new tools, and never stop learning.

Collaboration

The best ideas grow when people build together, not alone.

Consistency

Small, steady progress beats short bursts of motivation.

Community

Everyone has something to teach and something to learn.
